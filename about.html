Our mission

Everyone deserves to hear the story.

StoryCast publishes documentary audio and film from the edges of ordinary life and builds every piece so that no listener, viewer or reader is left outside it.

Independent, paid, and made with care

We commission independent producers and first-time storytellers from around the world. Every piece is paid, edited with care, and published with captions, transcripts and descriptions from day one, not added later.

We are listener-supported and ad-free, so the work can stay slow, specific and human.

How StoryCast is built for everyone

  • Captions on every video

    Synchronised, accurate captions are included with every film, in the original language and English.

  • Transcripts for every story

    Full text transcripts with speaker labels and timestamps sit beside the player and can be downloaded.

  • Keyboard first

    Every control is reachable and operable with the keyboard, with a skip link and clear visible focus.

How to engage with the stories

  1. Listen or watch

    Press play on any story. Captions are on by default; toggle them with the CC button.

  2. Read along

    Open the transcript beside the player to follow, jump to a moment, or download the full text.

  3. Tell us what is missing
