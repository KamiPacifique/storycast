Night Frequencies

A radio repairman in a midnight market keeps the city's forgotten stations alive.

By Daniel Okafor

Full transcript

Download .txt

  1. Narrator

    After midnight, the market belongs to the radio repairman.

  2. Narrator

    Every shelf hums. Every dial points to a station that closed years ago.

  3. Emeka

    People bring me dead radios. I bring back the voices inside.

  4. [Sound]

    Static swells, then clears into a faint dance-band melody.

  5. Narrator

    Some nights a stranger waits an hour just to hear one song from his childhood.

  6. Emeka

    That is the job. Not fixing the machine. Giving the memory somewhere to land.
