How to Share an Excel Workbook Online

A step-by-step screen recording: open Excel on the web, choose who can view or edit, and send the link.

By StoryCast Studio

Full transcript

  1. On screen

    Title card: “How to share an Excel workbook online”.

  2. On screen

    In a web browser, the address office.com/launch/excel is typed in. The same address appears as text on the screen.

  3. On screen

    Excel for the web opens. Under “Create new” are Blank workbook, Take a tour, Simple list and Premium templates. Below, recently opened files are listed.

  4. On screen

    A workbook named M15.xlsx is open. The “Link settings” panel shows who the link works for (Anyone, Only people with existing access, or People you choose), a permission set to “Can edit”, optional expiry date and password, and an Apply button.

  5. On screen

    The “Share M15.xlsx” dialog opens. An email address has been added as a recipient.

  6. On screen

    A short message is typed into the dialog. The note beneath reads “Anyone with the link can edit.” The cursor rests on the Send button.

  7. On screen

    Closing animation: a blue keyboard graphic.
