Terraces at First Light

Three generations of tea pickers on the hills they have shaped by hand.

By Priya Raman

Full transcript

Download .txt

  1. Narrator

    At five in the morning the hills are still blue.

  2. Narrator

    Three generations climb the same terraces, each one holding a basket.

  3. Lakshmi

    My grandmother cut these steps with a hoe. I only walk where she made a path. (Translated from Tamil)

  4. [Sound]

    Leaves rustle. Baskets knock softly against hips.

  5. Narrator

    The youngest, Meena, is learning which two leaves and a bud to pick.

  6. Meena

    It is slow work. But when the sun comes over the ridge, nobody wants to be anywhere else. (Translated from Tamil)
